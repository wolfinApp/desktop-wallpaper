## Desktop Wallpaper: A Simple Guide to 8K Wallpapers

Six wallpapers for your desktop, each carrying a short line of Arabic. They're free, there's no account to make, and every one downloads at full 8K, so it stays sharp on whatever screen you put it on.

## 1. Open the wallpapers

Find Desktop Wallpaper on the Services page and choose **Browse**, or go straight to [wolfin.app/wallpapers](https://wolfin.app/wallpapers). Every wallpaper is there as a small preview, with its name and the size of the file.

![Wallpaper with the line إن مع العسر يسرا](https://wolfin.app/assets/wallpapers/wallpaper-01-thumb.jpg)

*إن مع العسر يسرا. Each wallpaper is named after the line written on it.*

## 2. Pick one and download it

Choose **Download** under the one you like. You get a PNG at 7680×4320 pixels, between 18 and 35 MB, so give it a moment on a slow connection. The preview on the page is a small copy; the download is the full original.

![Wallpaper with the line وقل رب زدني علما](https://wolfin.app/assets/wallpapers/wallpaper-04-thumb.jpg)

*وقل رب زدني علما. 25 MB at full size.*

## 3. Set it as your wallpaper

**On a Mac:** right-click the file in Finder and choose Set Desktop Picture. You can also open System Settings, then Wallpaper, and add it from there.

**On Windows:** right-click the file and choose Set as desktop background. Or open Settings, then Personalization, then Background, and choose Browse photos.

The wallpapers are 16:9. If your screen is a little taller, like most MacBooks, choose Fill (Fill Screen on a Mac) so the picture covers the whole screen.

## Why 8K

A 4K or 5K screen shows every pixel, so a smaller picture gets stretched and turns soft. At 8K there's more than enough for the biggest desktop screens today, and your computer scales it down without losing any detail.

## On a phone

These are made for wide desktop screens. You can download one on a phone, but the file is large and the picture is wide, so a phone crops most of it away.
